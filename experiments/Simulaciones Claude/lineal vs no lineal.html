No lineal: ẋ = v·cos(α+θ), ẏ = v·sin(α+θ), θ̇ = (v₀/b)·tan δ, con α = arctan(a·tan δ / b) y v = v₀ / cos α.
Linealizado (5.35): ẏ = v₀·θ + (a·v₀/b)·δ, θ̇ = (v₀/b)·δ, con avance x = v₀·t.
